About

We're Climate Resilient Youth, a youth-led project bridging climate inequity gaps through education and technology.

Climate Resilient Youth was founded in 2026 by Anya, who wanted to create educational resources to teach youth how to prepare for natural disasters and other climate-related issues. Through the Climate Leaders Fellowship and NOAA Ocean Guardian Youth Ambassador Program, she was able to launch this project with support, and as a Kode With Klossy scholar and Girls Who Code Pathways student, was able to learn and code this website.
