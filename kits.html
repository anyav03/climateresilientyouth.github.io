Disaster Supply Kits

Graphics from Canva.

Disaster supply kits are built to last at least 72 hours. This is because in an emergency, you must be prepared to stay at your current location for multiple days at a time. Disaster supply kits are also built for easy travel, especially for taking quickly in an emergency.

Disaster supply kits can be costly, especially if there are multiple people you’re packing for. Here are some tips for low cost disaster supply kits that are simultaneously earth-conscious.

  • Use what you already have at home.
  • Slowly build up your kit. For example, buy 1 item from the store when you go grocery shopping)
  • Fit 2 people in 1 bag (ex: children).
  • Use a bucket instead of bag (can also be used as a toilet).
