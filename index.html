Climate Resilient Youth

Advocating for climate equity globally and inspiring youth to take action.

Youth complete courses and earn certificates to become Climate Resilient in the fields of their choice.

Hurricane Helene on September 26, 2024. (Image credit: NOAA/NEDIS/STAR GOES East)

Natural disasters effect people all across the globe. They can completely flip peoples' lives upside down. Climate Resilient Youth's mission is to be apart of the action to help reduce the effects of climate change through education and technology, especially for youth.

Why youth?

Youth are the driving force of action and the next generation to make real change. By providing resources, we push youth to step out of their comfort zone and learn about natural disaster preparedness and climate change.
